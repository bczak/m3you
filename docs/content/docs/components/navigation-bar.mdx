---
title: Navigation bar
description: Bottom navigation between three to five destinations.
---

Navigation bars switch between the top-level destinations of an app on compact
screens. Use between three and five — fewer and tabs would do, more and the
targets get too small.

Each item needs a `value`, an `icon` and a `label`. Pass `activeIcon` to swap in
a filled icon when selected, which is the Material convention.

Every item responds to taps across the bar's full 64dp height. That includes
`orientation="horizontal"`, where only a 40dp pill is drawn. The bottom safe-area
inset the bar adds below the items does not respond to taps.
On a vertical item the focus ring sits inside the item. On a horizontal item it
is a round ring 2dp outside the pill.
