---
title: Extendable FAB
description: A FAB that expands to reveal its label, then collapses again.
---

The extendable FAB starts as an icon-only FAB and grows into an extended FAB
with a label. The typical trigger is scroll direction: extended at the top of a
list, collapsed once the user scrolls down.

Both `icon` and `label` are required — the component needs them to measure the
extended width before animating to it.

## States

Every variant rests at elevation level 3, rises to level 4 on hover and drops
back to level 3 while pressed; `lowered` uses levels 1, 2 and 1. Keyboard focus
adds a 10% state layer under the focus ring. A disabled FAB looks the same
whatever its `variant` or `color`: an on-surface 10% container, 38% content and
no shadow. The outlined variant keeps its stroke.

## Accessibility

`label` names the button in both states, through `aria-labelledby`, so a
collapsed FAB is still announced by its label. This also works when the label is
an element rather than a string, such as a translated message. Pass
`aria-label` (or your own `aria-labelledby`) when the name should differ from
the label.
