---
title: Connected button group
description: Tightly spaced segments whose corners round as selection moves.
---

The segments sit 2px apart. The selected segment rounds fully while its
neighbours square off, and the radii animate as selection moves — this is the
clearest demonstration of why the library never uses `border-radius: 9999px`,
which cannot interpolate.

Like the standard group, selection is by index.

The group adds no padding around its segments, so it is exactly as tall as its
buttons. At `xs` and `sm` each segment still carries its own 48px touch target.

## Corners

Every corner where two segments meet uses the group's **inner radius** — 4, 8, 8,
16 and 20px from `xs` to `xl`. The two outer ends depend on `shape`:

- `round` (default) — the outer ends are pills.
- `square` — the outer ends use the inner radius too, so every unselected
  segment has the same corners.

A selected segment rounds fully in both shapes. An unselected segment raises its
inner radius one step while it is hovered, focused or pressed — to 12, 12, 16, 28
and 32px — and springs back on release. A round group's pill ends stay put, and
disabled segments keep their resting corners.
