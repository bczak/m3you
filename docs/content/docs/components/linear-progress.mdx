import { LinearProgress } from 'm3you';

Linear progress suits work attached to a region of the screen — a page load, an
upload, a form submission. It stretches to the width of its container.

The active indicator stops a 4dp gap short of the track, and the track runs to
the end of the bar with the stop dot sitting on its rounded end. In the
indeterminate state the track splits around the moving segment, keeping the
4dp gap on both sides, and the segment widens from about a third to about two
thirds of the bar as it travels.

## Wavy

The wave swings 3px either side of its centre line at both thicknesses — only
the stroke grows — so a wavy bar is 10px tall at 4dp and 14px at 8dp. It keeps
that height at every value: at 10% and below, and at 100%, the line lies flat
inside the same box, so finishing does not shift the layout. The determinate
wave has a 40px wavelength and the indeterminate one 20px, and both ends of the
wave are drawn with round caps. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the wave
holds still; an indeterminate segment still travels along the track.
