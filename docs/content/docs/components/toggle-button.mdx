A toggle button represents a setting that is on or off, rather than an action
that happens once. Use `selected` with `onSelectedChange` to control it, or
`defaultSelected` to let it manage itself.

## Selection

Selecting a toggle swaps its shape as well as its colour: a round button takes
square corners and a square one becomes a pill. Elevated toggles keep their
size's elevation in both states, and an outlined toggle trades its stroke for an
inverse-surface fill — the stroke fades out with the fill rather than snapping.

## Morph

While a toggle is held its corners tighten to a smaller radius — the Expressive
press gesture. `morph` is on by default; pass `morph={false}` to keep the
resting shape. It runs on the spring curve, so it settles rather than snapping.
