A split button pairs one obvious action with a menu of variations on it. Compose
it from `SplitButtonAction` for the leading half and `SplitButtonMenu` for the
trailing chevron; both inherit `variant`, `size` and `shape` from the root.

<Example
  name="split-button/basic"
  title="Save, with alternatives"
  description="The trailing half opens a menu."
/>

## Shape and morph

The two halves share a 2dp gap. Their outer corners follow `shape`; the inner
corners stay small so the pair reads as one control. Hovering or pressing a half
grows its two inner corners — 4 to 8dp at `xs`, 4 to 12dp at `sm` and `md`, 8
to 20dp at `lg` and 12 to 20dp at `xl` — and the outer corners stay put. While
the menu is open the trigger becomes fully round. `morph` is on by default; pass
`morph={false}` to keep the inner corners still.

<Example
  name="split-button/morph"
  title="Inner-corner morph"
  description="Hover or press either half. The second has morph={false} and keeps its shape."
/>

## Disabled

`disabled` disables both halves. Outlined halves drop to an On Surface stroke
at 10% with no container fill, the other variants to a 10% On Surface container.
