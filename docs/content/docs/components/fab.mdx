The floating action button carries the single most important action on a screen.
There should be at most one per screen, and it should never be the only way to
reach that action.

## Shape

The corners follow the size: 16dp on the 56dp FAB, 20dp on the 80dp medium
FAB and 28dp on the 96dp large FAB. A FAB keeps its corner radius when pressed.
Unlike an icon button it does not morph by default; pass `morph` to opt in.

## Elevation

Every FAB rests at elevation level 3, whatever its `variant` or size. It rises to
level 4 on hover and drops back to level 3 while pressed. `lowered` moves it down
to level 1 (level 2 on hover), for a FAB that sits on a coloured surface. A
disabled FAB is flat.
