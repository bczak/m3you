import { CircularProgress } from 'm3you';

Use determinate progress when you know how much work is left, and indeterminate
when you do not. The `wavy` variant is the Material 3 Expressive treatment — the
active indicator ripples while the track stays flat.

The active indicator and the track are separated by a 4dp gap at each end, and
in the indeterminate state the track follows the moving arc. At `value={0}`
only the track is drawn; at `value={100}` only the indicator is drawn, and the
wavy ring stays wavy.

## Thickness

`thickness` takes the kit's 4dp or 8dp stroke. The ring keeps its radius and the
box grows with the stroke: a medium flat indicator is 40px at 4dp and 44px at
8dp, and a medium wavy one is 48px and 52px. The free-form `strokeWidth` follows
the same rule (box = 4dp size + stroke − 4).

<Example name="circular-progress/thickness" title="4dp and 8dp, flat and wavy" />

A `width` and `height` in `style`, or a stylesheet rule on your own
`className`, override the computed box on both variants, and the whole ring
scales to fill it: stroke, wave and gap together. The component applies its box
with a single-class rule, so yours wins when it loads after `m3you/styles.css`
or is more specific. Use `size` and `thickness` to stay on the kit's
measurements.
