Bottom sheets hold supplementary content anchored to the bottom edge. They are
built on Base UI's drawer primitive, so dragging, snap points and swipe-to-close
behave the way a native sheet does. The sheet spans the full width of compact
windows and is capped at 640px, centred, on wider ones.

## Modal and standard

A modal sheet (the default) dims the page with a 32% scrim, traps focus and
locks page scroll; a press on the scrim dismisses it. Set `modal={false}` for a
standard sheet: it has no scrim, lifts on an elevation-3 shadow, and only the
sheet surface catches pointer events, so the page behind stays usable. A press
on the page still dismisses a standard sheet by default — add
`disablePointerDismissal` to keep it open while the page is in use.

`modal="trap-focus"` keeps the modal scrim and look and traps focus, but does
not lock page scroll.

## Snap points and motion

Pass `snapPoints` to let the sheet rest part-way open. A number from 0 to 1 is
a fraction of the screen height; a larger number, or a `px` or `rem` string, is
a fixed height. `defaultSnapPoint` (or a controlled `snapPoint` with
`onSnapPointChange`) picks where it opens. Dragging the handle moves between
points, and dragging below the lowest point dismisses the sheet.

The sheet slides up through the bottom edge to open, glides between snap
points, and slides back down to close; after a swipe the exit continues from
where the finger let go. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it does not
travel: it opens in place at its snap point and fades with the scrim, closes by
fading where it is (even from a higher snap point, or from where a swipe let
go), and snap changes jump instead of gliding.
