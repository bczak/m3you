---
title: Bottom sheet
description: A swipeable surface anchored to the bottom of the screen.
---

Bottom sheets hold supplementary content anchored to the bottom edge. They are
built on Base UI's drawer primitive, so dragging, snap points and swipe-to-close
behave the way a native sheet does.
